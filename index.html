    Filing a complaint offline

    For when there's no portal access, no smartphone, or you just prefer paper
    Step by step
    • Generate the PDF from Aawedhan Saathi as usual — it's written as a formal application, so it's ready to print and use offline too.
    • Print two copies: one to submit, one for your own record.
    • Sign both copies by hand at the bottom, above your typed name.
    • Visit the office of the authority named on the application (shown on the detail page for your category) during working hours.
    • Hand it to the receiving clerk / public grievance counter and insist on a dated acknowledgement — either a stamp and signature on your copy, or a receipt slip with a reference number.
    • If the office refuses to accept it or there's no receiving counter, send it by Registered Post / Speed Post with Acknowledgement Due (AD) — the postal receipt itself becomes your proof of submission and date.
    • Note the reference number or postal tracking ID somewhere safe — you'll need it to follow up.
    • If there's no response within a reasonable time (commonly 30 days), escalate in writing to the next officer up the chain, attaching a copy of your first application and receipt as proof it was filed.
    If you're unsure who to submit to
    Any Citizen Facilitation Centre / Jan Suvidha Kendra can guide you to the right department
